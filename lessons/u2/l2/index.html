Wages and Salaries: Gross Pay and Pay Periods

How much do you earn before anything is taken off? Today we work out gross pay for jobs paid by the hour (a wage) and jobs paid by the year (a salary), check Alberta’s minimum wage, split a salary into paycheques, and compare job offers fairly.

1

Wages, Salaries and Gross Pay

Your income is the money you are paid for the work you do. Two of the most common ways to be paid are:

WageYou are paid a set amount for each hour you work (an hourly wage or hourly rate, like $17.50/h). More hours means more pay.
SalaryYou are paid a set amount for a whole year (like $52 000 a year), split into equal paycheques. The pay stays the same even if you work a little more or less one week.
Gross pay is everything you earn before anything is taken off. Your employer takes money off your cheque for things like income tax. These are called deductions. The amount you actually take home is your net pay, so net pay is always less than gross pay. (Deductions are in Lesson 7.)
Jobs that often pay a wageJobs that often pay a salary
cashier, cook, server, warehouse worker, lifeguard, construction labourer, apprentice in a tradeteacher, office manager, accountant, engineer, store manager, police officer

Gross Pay from an Hourly Wage

To find gross pay for an hourly job, multiply the hours by the hourly wage.

Gross pay from a wage: gross pay = hours worked \times hourly wage
Write the hours as a decimal first. Round the pay to the nearest cent at the end.
↺
Quick refresher: minutes ÷ 60 = part of an hour. 15 min = 0.25 h, 30 min = 0.5 h, 45 min = 0.75 h. So 7 h 45 min = 7.75 h, not 7.45 h.
≈
Estimate: round the hours and the wage to easy numbers. For 7.75 h at $23.85/h: about 8\times\$24=\$192. If your calculator says $1848 or $18.48, something went wrong.
Example 1

Hours × hourly wage

(a) Noor works at a garden centre for $16.40/h. On Saturday she works 6 hours. What is her gross pay?
gross pay =6\times\$16.40=\$98.40
Noor earns $98.40.
(b) Dev is an apprentice carpenter. He earns $23.85/h. One day he works 7 h 45 min. Estimate, then find his gross pay.
Estimate: 8\times\$24=\$192
7 h 45 min = 7.75 h
gross pay =7.75\times\$23.85=\$184.8375\approx\$184.84
Dev earns $184.84 (close to the estimate).

Pay from a timesheet

Most hourly workers fill in a timesheet (or time card). It shows when each shift starts and ends. A break you are not paid for (an unpaid break) is taken off the hours.

Example 2

Sofia’s week

Sofia is a cook at a café in Cochrane. She earns $17.60/h. Complete her timesheet, then find her gross pay for the week.

DayStartEndUnpaid breakHours paid
Monday6:00 a.m.2:30 p.m.30 min8
Tuesday7:00 a.m.12:00 p.m.none5
Wednesdayday off0
Thursday10:30 a.m.4:45 p.m.30 min5.75
Friday11:00 a.m.7:30 p.m.30 min8
Saturday8:00 a.m.12:00 p.m.none4

Gross pay for the week:
Mon: 6:00 to 2:30 is 8 h 30 min = 8.5 h; 8.5-0.5=8 h. Thu: 10:30 to 4:45 is 6 h 15 min = 6.25 h; 6.25-0.5=5.75 h. Fri: 8.5-0.5=8 h
total =8+5+5.75+8+4=30.75 h
gross pay =30.75\times\$17.60=\$541.20 (estimate: 31\times\$18=\$558 ✓)
Sofia’s gross pay for the week is $541.20.

Finding the hourly wage

If you know the pay and the hours, you can find the hourly wage. Undo the multiplying: divide.

Finding the hourly wage: hourly wage = gross pay \div hours worked
Example 3

Amara’s hourly wage

Amara works at a pharmacy. Last week she worked 25.5 hours and her gross pay was $446.25. What is her hourly wage? Check your answer.
hourly wage =\$446.25\div25.5=\$17.50
Check: 25.5\times\$17.50=\$446.25 ✓
Amara earns $17.50/h.

Alberta’s Minimum Wage

The minimum wage is the lowest hourly wage an employer is allowed to pay. Each province sets its own.

Alberta minimum wage (as of 2026; rates can change)
$15.00/hMost workers: at least $15.00 an hour.
$13.00/hStudents under 18 can be paid $13.00/h for the first 28 hours in a week while school is in session. Any hours past 28 that week, and all hours in the summer or other school breaks, must be paid at least $15.00/h.

To check a job, find the hourly wage (pay ÷ hours) and compare it with the minimum.

Example 4

Minimum wage or not?

Is each person paid at least Alberta’s minimum wage?

(a) Ben is 22. He earned $456.00 for 32 hours at a car dealership.
\$456.00\div32=\$14.25/h
\$14.25<\$15.00
No. Ben is paid less than minimum wage.
(b) Lucy is 16. In November she earned $283.50 for 21 hours at a bakery.
\$283.50\div21=\$13.50/h
School is in, she is under 18 and works under 28 h, so the minimum is $13.00.
Yes, \$13.50\ge\$13.00.
(c) In July, Lucy earned $378.00 for 28 hours at the same bakery.
\$378.00\div28=\$13.50/h
In the summer the minimum is $15.00.
No. \$13.50<\$15.00.
Example 5

Tyler’s week in October

Tyler is 17 and works at a sporting goods store. His employer pays the lowest wage the law allows. In one week in October, Tyler works 31 hours. What is his gross pay?
first 28 h at $13.00: 28\times\$13.00=\$364.00
hours past 28: 31-28=3 h at $15.00: 3\times\$15.00=\$45.00
gross pay =\$364.00+\$45.00=\$409.00
Tyler’s gross pay is $409.00.

Salaries and Pay Periods

A salary is a yearly amount, but nobody waits a whole year to be paid. The salary is split into equal paycheques. The time each cheque covers is a pay period.

Pay periodHow often you are paidPaycheques in a year
weeklyevery week52
biweeklyevery two weeks26
semi-monthlytwice a month (e.g. the 15th and the last day)24
monthlyonce a month12
Salaries: pay per cheque = yearly salary \div number of pay periods
yearly salary = pay per cheque \times number of pay periods
!
Biweekly (every 2 weeks, 26 cheques) and semi-monthly (twice a month, 24 cheques) sound alike but are different. A month is a bit more than 4 weeks, so 26 is more than 2\times12=24.
Example 6

Paycheques from a salary

Hana is a dental office manager. Her salary is $54 600 a year.

(a) Complete the table to show her gross pay for each pay period.

Calculation54\,600\div5254\,600\div2654\,600\div2454\,600\div12
Gross pay$1 050.00$2 100.00$2 275.00$4 550.00
(b) Marco is a lab technician. He is paid $1 925.50 biweekly. What is his yearly salary? What is his average pay per month?
yearly salary =\$1\,925.50\times26=\$50\,063.00
per month =\$50\,063.00\div12=\$4\,171.9166\ldots\approx\$4\,171.92
Marco earns $50 063.00 a year, which is about $4 171.92 a month.

The hourly equivalent of a salary

To compare a salary with an hourly wage, find the salary’s hourly equivalent: what it works out to for each hour worked. A full-time 40-hour week is 40\times52=2\,080 hours a year.

Hourly equivalent of a salary: hourly equivalent = yearly salary \div (hours per week \times 52)
Yearly pay from a wage: yearly pay = hourly wage \times hours per week \times weeks worked
On the TI-30XIIS: for $46 800 and 40 h a week, key 46800÷(40×52)ENTER. It gives 22.5. Without the brackets the calculator divides by 40 and then multiplies by 52.
Example 7

Per hour and per year

(a) A youth centre pays its program coordinator a salary of $46 800 for a 40-hour week. What is the hourly equivalent?
hours a year =40\times52=2\,080
\$46\,800\div2\,080=\$22.50
The salary works out to $22.50/h.
(b) Jesse works at a print shop for $21.40/h, 37.5 hours every week of the year. What is his yearly gross pay?
\$21.40\times37.5\times52=\$41\,730.00
Jesse earns $41 730.00 a year.

Comparing Wage and Salary Jobs

To compare two jobs fairly, put them in the same units: pay per week (or per year) and pay per hour. Then think about more than the money.

Hourly wageSalary
Goodpaid for every hour you work; extra hours mean extra pay (and overtime)the same pay every cheque, so it is easy to budget; often paid vacation and benefits
Not so goodhours can be cut, so pay can change; no work means no payextra hours usually mean no extra pay
Example 8

Riya’s two job offers

Riya has two job offers. Job A: $22.75/h, 35 hours a week. Job B: a salary of $42 900 a year, 37.5 hours a week.

(a) Find the weekly pay for each job.
A: 35\times\$22.75=\$796.25
B: \$42\,900\div52=\$825.00
Job B pays $28.75 more a week.
(b) Find the pay per hour for each job.
A: $22.75/h
B: \$825.00\div37.5=\$22.00/h
Job A pays more for each hour.
(c) What if Job A gave Riya 40 hours a week instead? Which job would pay more each week then?
A: 40\times\$22.75=\$910.00, which is more than $825.00. Job A would pay more.
(d) Which job would you pick? Give one reason about money and one other reason.
Sample: Job B, because it pays $825.00 a week no matter what and it is easy to budget.
Or Job A: it pays more per hour, leaves 2.5 h more free time, and extra hours are paid.
Example 9

Find the mistake

Find the mistake in each solution and fix it.

(a) Cole’s salary is $39 000 a year, paid semi-monthly.
Cole’s work: \$39\,000\div26=\$1\,500.00 per cheque.
Mistake: semi-monthly means 24 cheques, not 26 (26 is biweekly).
\$39\,000\div24=\$1\,625.00
Each cheque is $1 625.00.
(b) Erin worked 6 h 45 min at $16.00/h.
Erin’s work: 6.45\times\$16.00=\$103.20.
Mistake: 45 min is not 0.45 h. 45\div60=0.75, so 6 h 45 min = 6.75 h.
6.75\times\$16.00=\$108.00
Erin earned $108.00.
✓

Key ideas

Wage: paid by the hour. Salary: paid by the year. Gross pay is before deductions; net pay (what you take home) is less.
Gross pay = hours × hourly wage (hours as a decimal: 7 h 45 min = 7.75 h). Hourly wage = pay ÷ hours. Estimate first; round to the cent at the end.
Alberta minimum wage: $15.00/h; students under 18 may get $13.00/h for the first 28 h a week while school is in.
Pay periods: weekly 52, biweekly 26, semi-monthly 24, monthly 12. Cheque = salary ÷ periods. Hourly equivalent = salary ÷ (hours per week × 52).
