Warm-up

A Pound of Feathers or a Pound of Gold?

Here is a very old riddle.

One bag holds 1 pound of feathers. A jeweller has 1 pound of gold.

Which is heavier: the pound of feathers or the pound of gold?

A the feathers
B the gold
C they are the same
A, the feathers! (Really.) Everyone knows the trick answer “the same”. But gold is weighed with an old unit, the troy pound: about 373 g. Everything else uses the regular pound: about 454 g. So the pound of feathers is heavier, by about 81 g. Bonus twist: an ounce of gold (a troy ounce, about 31.1 g) is heavier than an ounce of feathers (about 28.35 g)!

Your bathroom scale says pounds. The airline says kilograms. The flyer says $/lb and the till says $/kg. Today we change pounds and ounces to kilograms and grams and back: in our heads, and exactly.

The Conversion Facts and Quick Estimates

In Canada, most things are labelled in grams and kilograms. But many people still use pounds (lb) and ounces (oz): bathroom scales, gym plates, newborn babies, US recipes and some flyers. To change between the two systems, you need a few conversion facts. They are approximate (the symbol ≈ means “is about”).

Key idea
Conversion factsInside each system (exact)Referents
1 lb ≈ 0.454 kg (= 454 g)1 lb = 16 oza 1 lb block of butter is 454 g
1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb1 ton = 2000 lba 1 kg bag of sugar is a bit more than 2 lb
1 oz ≈ 28.35 g1 kg = 1000 ga slice of bread is about 1 oz (about 30 g)
1 ton ≈ 0.907 t1 tonne (t) = 1000 kga ton is a bit less than a tonne

In everyday speech people say weight when they mean mass (“my bag weighs 20 kg”). In this lesson the two words mean the same thing. A ton here is the short ton (2000 lb). Do not mix it up with a tonne (t), which is 1000 kg.

Which is heavier? Change one mass to the other’s unit

To compare a mass in one system with a mass in the other, change one of them so both are in the same unit. Then the bigger number is the heavier mass.

Think Is 1 tonne more or less than 2000 lb?
1 t = 1000 kg ≈ 1000\times2.2=2200 lb. That is more than 2000 lb (a ton). So a tonne is a bit more than a ton.

Quick estimates in your head

A kilogram is a bit more than 2 pounds. A pound is a bit less than half a kilogram.

Key idea
kg → lb: double it, then add 10% of that.20 kg: 20\times2=40,  40+4=44 lb
lb → kg: halve it, then take away 10% of that.120 lb: 120\div2=60,  60-6=54 kg
oz → g: about 30 g for each ounce.5 oz ≈ 5\times30=150 g
Think Why is “double it, then add 10%” the same as multiplying by 2.2?
Doubling gives 2 times the number. 10% of that is 0.2 times the number. Together: 2+0.2=2.2 times the number. So the estimate for kg → lb is exact!

Converting with a Unit Factor

For an exact answer, multiply by a unit factor: a fraction made from a conversion fact, like \dfrac{0.454\text{ kg}}{1\text{ lb}}. Its top and bottom are the same mass, so it equals 1. Multiplying by it changes the units but not the mass.

Key idea
  1. Step 1. Write the amount you have, with its unit.
  2. Step 2. Multiply by a unit factor with the unit you have on the bottom and the unit you want on the top.
  3. Step 3. Cross out the unit that is on the top and the bottom. Calculate, then round.
  4. Step 4. Check with a quick estimate.
25 lb to kilograms: 25\ \cancel{\text{lb}}\times\dfrac{0.454\text{ kg}}{1\ \cancel{\text{lb}}}=25\times0.454=11.35\text{ kg}
Check: half of 25 is 12.5, take away 1.25: about 11.3 kg.
500 g to ounces: 500\ \cancel{\text{g}}\times\dfrac{1\text{ oz}}{28.35\ \cancel{\text{g}}}=500\div28.35\approx17.6\text{ oz}
The fact is on the bottom, so you divide.

You can also use a proportion, as in Unit 1: \dfrac{1\text{ lb}}{0.454\text{ kg}}=\dfrac{25\text{ lb}}{x} gives x=25\times0.454=11.35 kg.

!
Watch out. Ask: should the number get bigger or smaller? A kilogram is bigger than a pound, so you need more pounds than kilograms: 10 kg is about 22 lb, not about 4.5 lb. A gram is tiny, so a mass in grams is a much bigger number than the same mass in ounces.
25 × 0.454 ENTER shows 11.35. 500 ÷ 28.35 ENTER shows 17.63668…, about 17.6.

Why does a unit factor work?

A block of butter is 1 lb, and it is also 0.454 kg: the same mass, written two ways. So \dfrac{0.454\text{ kg}}{1\text{ lb}} is like \dfrac{6}{6}: the top and the bottom are the same amount, and the fraction equals 1.

Multiplying by 1 never changes an amount. It only changes how we write it. And units cancel just like numbers: \dfrac{\cancel{\text{lb}}\cdot\text{kg}}{\cancel{\text{lb}}}=\text{kg}.

Think Sam wrote 25\text{ lb}\times\dfrac{1\text{ lb}}{0.454\text{ kg}}. What goes wrong?
The pounds don’t cancel: they end up as lb × lb ÷ kg, which is not a mass. The unit you have (lb) must be on the bottom.

Bigger or smaller? Count the blocks

Line up kilogram blocks and pound blocks under the same mass. Each kilogram is about 2.2 pounds, so it takes more pound blocks.

Pounds and ounces

A mass like 7 lb 6 oz has two units. Change it to one unit first: either all pounds (ounces ÷ 16) or all ounces (pounds × 16, then add the ounces).

!
Watch out. 7 lb 6 oz is not 7.6 lb. There are 16 oz in a pound, not 10. 6 oz = 6\div16=0.375 lb, so 7 lb 6 oz = 7.375 lb.
Use brackets: ( 7 + 6 ÷ 16 ) × 0.454 ENTER shows 3.34825.

Baby Amara and the dog food

(a) Baby Amara was born at 7 lb 6 oz. Find her mass in kilograms, to the nearest hundredth, two ways.

(i) Change to pounds, then to kilograms.
6 oz = 6\div16=0.375 lb, so 7.375 lb
7.375\times0.454=3.348\ldots kg
Amara’s mass is about 3.35 kg.

7\times16+6=118 oz

3345.3\div1000=3.3453\approx3.35 kg (the same)
(b) A bag of dog food is 9 kg. Find its mass in pounds and ounces, to the nearest ounce.
9\times2.2=19.8 lb, which is 19 lb plus 0.8 lb
0.8\times16=12.8\approx13 oz
The bag is about 19 lb 13 oz.

Limits: Baggage, Shipping and Loads

Airlines, couriers, trucks and bridges all have mass limits. Often the limit is in one system and your scale is in the other. Convert so both masses are in the same unit, then compare.

↓
Round down for “how many”. If the question asks how many whole items fit under a limit, round down. 24.8 bags means only 24 bags fit: the 25th bag would go over.
Think A bridge sign says “Maximum 5 t”. A loaded truck weighs 12 000 lb. Can it cross?
12\,000\times0.454=5448 kg = 5.448 t. That is more than 5 t, so no. (Estimate: half of 12 000 is 6000, take away 600: about 5400 kg.)
Example 4

Tamika’s suitcase

An airline lets each passenger check one bag of up to 23 kg. Tamika’s bathroom scale shows her suitcase at 53 lb.

(a) Estimate: is her bag over the limit?
Halve: 53\div2=26.5
Take away 10%: 26.5-2.65\approx23.9 kg
Yes, it looks a little over 23 kg.
(b) Find the bag’s mass in kilograms, to the nearest tenth. By how much is it over?
53\times0.454=24.062 kg ≈ 24.1 kg
24.062-23=1.062
It is about 1.1 kg over the limit.
(c) Her carry-on bag may be up to 7 kg. It now weighs 11 lb. Can she move 1.5 kg of things from the suitcase into the carry-on and still be within both limits?
Carry-on now: 11\times0.454=4.994\approx5.0 kg
After moving: carry-on 5.0+1.5=6.5 kg, less than 7 kg. Suitcase 24.1-1.5=22.6 kg, less than 23 kg.
Yes, both bags will be within the limits.

Shipping: the price depends on the mass

A courier’s price table has steps. Find the total mass of the parcel in the table’s unit, then find the row it lands in. A parcel exactly on a limit (“up to 1 kg”) still pays the lower price.

Marco’s gravel

A rental dump trailer can carry at most 3 t (tonnes). Gravel has a mass of about 1.4 tons per cubic yard.

(a) Can the trailer safely carry 2 cubic yards of gravel?
Mass: 2\times1.4=2.8 tons

2.54 t is less than 3 t, so yes, it can.

Math HistoryAir Canada 143 · 23 July 1983

The Gimli Glider

In 1983 Canada was switching to metric. Air Canada’s new Boeing 767 measured its fuel in kilograms. Flight 143, Montreal to Edmonton, needed 22 300 kg of fuel. The fuel gauges were broken, so the crew worked it out by hand.

They used a pounds fact by mistake. The jet took off with 22 300 lb of fuel. Both engines stopped at 41 000 ft over Manitoba. The pilots glided to an old air force base at Gimli, and all 69 people on board survived.

The jet had 22 300 lb of fuel. About how many kilograms is that? How much was missing?
22\,300\times0.454\approx10\,100 kg: less than half! It was missing about 22\,300-10\,100=12\,200 kg of fuel.

Buying by the Pound or by the Kilogram

Meat, fish, cheese and produce are often priced per pound in flyers ($/lb) and per kilogram at the till ($/kg). To compare, change both to the same unit price.

A kilogram is about 2.2 pounds, so 1 kg costs about 2.2 times as much as 1 lb. The price per kg is always the bigger number.

Key idea   price per kg = price per lb × 2.2      price per lb = price per kg ÷ 2.2
Think A flyer shows chicken at “$3.90/lb” in big print and “$8.60/kg” in small print. Do the two prices match?
3.90\times2.2=8.58. The store used a more exact factor (2.2046…), so $8.60/kg is the same price. Yes, they match.
!
Watch out. Converting a price goes the opposite way to converting a mass. To change 5 lb to kg you multiply by 0.454, but to change $5.00/lb to $/kg you multiply by 2.2. Think: “a kilogram is more meat, so it costs more.” Stores use a more exact factor, so their numbers may be a cent or two different from yours.
5.49 × 2.2 ENTER shows 12.078: about $12.08 per kg. 14.30 ÷ 2.2 ENTER shows 6.5: $6.50 per lb.

The volleyball barbecue

The volleyball team is having a barbecue. They need 30 quarter-pound burger patties (each patty is \tfrac14 lb of ground beef). Ground beef costs $12.00 per kg.

(c) How much will the beef cost?

Key ideas

The facts: 1 lb ≈ 0.454 kg, 1 kg ≈ 2.2 lb, 1 oz ≈ 28.35 g, 1 ton ≈ 0.907 t. Estimate: kg → lb double and add 10%; lb → kg halve and take away 10%; oz → g about 30 g each.
Unit factor: the unit you have on the bottom, the unit you want on top, cross out, calculate. Check: more pounds than kilograms; many more grams than ounces.
Pounds and ounces: change to one unit first. 6 oz = 6 ÷ 16 = 0.375 lb (not 0.6). Limits: put both masses in the same unit; round down for how many fit, up for packages or trips.
Prices: $/kg = $/lb × 2.2 and $/lb = $/kg ÷ 2.2. The price per kg is always the bigger number: a kilogram is more meat.
